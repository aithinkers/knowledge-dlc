# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good intent while evaluating evidence, accept constructive
feedback, protect private information, and focus disagreement on ideas and
observable behavior.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention,
doxxing, and disclosure of private project material are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns by emailing
[connect@aithinkers.com](mailto:connect@aithinkers.com?subject=K-DLC%20confidential%20conduct%20report)
with the subject `K-DLC confidential conduct report`. Initially provide only
your contact information and a non-sensitive summary; ask for a protected
channel before sending private evidence or identifying third parties. Do not
file a public issue for a conduct report.

Maintainers will limit the report to people needed for enforcement, disclose
conflicts of interest, and arrange an alternate handler when a report concerns
the normal recipient. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is
inappropriate. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented
privately, and protect reporter confidentiality. We aim to acknowledge reports
within 3 business days and provide an initial status within 10 business days.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
